## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit [SoulSync.com](https://soulsync.com)
2. Click "Join Now" or "Sign Up"
3. Fill in your basic information:
   - First name and last name
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Date of birth
   - Gender
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete your profile setup

### First Login
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You'll be directed to your dashboard

## Profile Setup

### Complete Profile Information

#### Basic Details
- **About Me**: Write a compelling bio (max 500 characters)
- **Location**: Current city and country
- **Education**: Highest qualification
- **Profession**: Job title and company
- **Religion**: Religious beliefs
- **Languages**: Languages you speak

#### Personal Preferences
- **Relationship Goals**: What you're looking for
- **Lifestyle**: Smoking, drinking preferences
- **Interests**: Hobbies and activities you enjoy
- **Values**: Important life values

#### Family Information
- **Family Background**: Brief family description
- **Family Type**: Nuclear, joint, etc.
- **Family Status**: Parents' marital status
- **Siblings**: Number and details

### Photo Guidelines

#### Profile Photos
- **Minimum**: 3 photos required
- **Recommended**: 5-7 photos for best results
- **Primary photo**: Clear face shot, good lighting
- **Additional photos**: Full body, lifestyle, interests

#### Photo Requirements
- **Quality**: High resolution (minimum 800x800)
- **Format**: JPEG, PNG, or WebP
- **Size**: Maximum 5MB per photo
- **Content**: Must show your face clearly

#### Photo Tips
✅ **Do:**
- Use recent photos (within 2 years)
- Show genuine smile
- Include variety (close-up, full body, activities)
- Use good lighting
- Dress appropriately

❌ **Don't:**
- Use heavily filtered photos
- Include other people prominently
- Use group photos as primary
- Post inappropriate content
- Use photos from social media with logos

### Horoscope Information (Optional)
- **Sun Sign**: Based on birth date
- **Moon Sign**: For detailed compatibility
- **Birth Time**: For accurate chart
- **Birth Place**: City of birth

## Finding Matches

### Browse Profiles
1. **Discover Section**: View suggested profiles
2. **Filter Options**: Age, location, education, etc.
3. **Search**: Advanced search with multiple criteria
4. **Recently Joined**: See newest members
5. **Premium/Verified**: Browse premium profiles

### Search Filters
- **Age Range**: Specify minimum and maximum age
- **Location**: City, state, or country
- **Education Level**: Qualification preferences
- **Profession**: Job categories
- **Religion**: Religious preferences
- **Marital Status**: Never married, divorced, etc.
- **Lifestyle**: Smoking, drinking preferences

### Profile Actions

#### Expressing Interest
- **Like**: Shows you're interested (👍)
- **Super Like**: Premium feature for strong interest (⭐)
- **Send Message**: Direct approach (premium)
- **Add to Favorites**: Save for later viewing

#### Profile Interaction
- **View Full Profile**: See complete information
- **View Photos**: Browse all uploaded photos
- **Compatibility Score**: See match percentage
- **Report Profile**: Flag inappropriate content

### Match Types

#### Mutual Matches
- Both users have liked each other
- Can start messaging immediately
- Appears in "Matches" section

#### One-Way Likes
- You liked them, waiting for response
- They liked you, you haven't responded
- Shown in separate sections

- **Public**: Visible to all users
- **Premium Only**: Visible to premium members

- **Invisible**: Browse without being seen (premium)

#### Photo Privacy
- **Public Photos**: Visible to all

### Safety Features

#### Verification
- **Email Verification**: Required for all accounts
- **Phone Verification**: Optional security measure
- **Photo Verification**: Confirms profile authenticity
- **Identity Verification**: Premium verification service

#### Reporting System
1. Click "Report" on any profile
2. Select reason for reporting
3. Provide additional details
4. Submit report
5. Our team reviews within 24 hours

#### Blocking Users
1. Go to user's profile
2. Click three dots menu
3. Select "Block User"
4. Confirm action
5. User is permanently blocked

### Meeting in Person

#### Safety Guidelines
- Meet in public places
- Tell friends about your plans
- Use your own transportation
- Trust your instincts
- Don't share personal information too quickly

#### First Date Tips
✅ **Recommended:**
- Coffee shops, restaurants, public parks
- Daytime meetings initially
- Bring a friend (double dates)
- Keep first dates short (1-2 hours)

❌ **Avoid:**
- Private homes or isolated locations
- Alcohol on first meetings
- Sharing too much personal information
- Feeling pressured to continue dating

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Problems
**Can't remember password:**
1. Click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email
3. Check your inbox
4. Follow reset instructions

**Account locked:**
- Wait 15 minutes for automatic unlock
- Contact support if persistent
- Check for unusual activity

#### Profile Issues
**Photos not uploading:**
- Check file size (max 5MB)
- Ensure supported format (JPEG, PNG, WebP)
- Check internet connection
- Try different browser

**Profile not showing in search:**
- Complete all required fields
- Upload at least 3 photos
- Verify email address
- Check privacy settings

#### Messaging Problems
**Messages not sending:**
- Check internet connection
- Verify recipient hasn't blocked you
- Ensure you have messaging privileges
- Contact support if persistent

**Not receiving notifications:**
- Check notification settings
- Verify email/phone number
- Check spam folder
- Update browser/app permissions

### FAQs

#### General Questions

**Q: Is SoulSync free to use?**
A: Yes, basic features are free. Premium features require subscription.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action is permanent.

**Q: Can I pause my profile?**
A: Yes, go to Settings > Privacy > Hide Profile temporarily.

**Q: How does matching work?**
A: Our algorithm considers preferences, compatibility, activity, and location.

#### Technical Questions

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions).

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Yes, available on iOS App Store and Google Play Store.

**Q: Can I use SoulSync on multiple devices?**
A: Yes, your account syncs across all devices.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use bank-level security and encryption.
